KnightVision: three art directions

The same four moments in each: the opening board, the twelve input sheets, the 4,096 outputs, and the mirror bug. All drawn from the network's real data.

  1. A · The WorkshopMaple pieces on green baize. Bauhaus forms, a wooden pegboard, pencil in a ledger.
  2. B · CorrespondenceA game by post. Risograph postcards, halftone ink, a stamp that prints mirrored.
  3. C · Op-Art NotebookBlack and white lab notebook. Ink outlines, mechanical loops, figures you can drag.
